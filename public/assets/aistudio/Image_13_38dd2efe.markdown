## Brand & Style
This design system embodies the high-fashion editorial gravitas of premier wedding cinematography and visual storytelling. It balances monumental Indian wedding traditions with modern European high-fashion editorial aesthetics. 

The aesthetic style merges **Editorial Minimalism** with **Cinematic Atmospheric Depth**:
- **Atmospheric & Immersive:** Dark canvas architecture mimicking the ambience of a cinema projection suite, allowing full-frame photography and film stills to radiate.
- **Editorial Asymmetry:** Rhythmic tension created through deliberate, magazine-style offset grids, varied typography scales, and breathing space.
- **Understated Opulence:** Restrained, precious champagne gold structural elements replacing ostentatious decorative patterns. Tactile, razor-thin lines suggest craftsmanship, precision lenses, and archival heirloom prints.

## Colors
The palette evokes archival gelatin silver prints, cinema auditoriums, and champagne light:

- **Primary Canvas (`#080808`):** Deep jet black providing absolute contrast and frame confinement for editorial imagery.
- **Secondary Canvas / Elevated Surfaces (`#101010`):** Charcoal tone used for floating cards, galleries, and overlay menus.
- **Accent Accent Gold (`#C6A477`):** Warm champagne gold reserved strictly for focal interactions, bespoke micro-borders, film roll stamps, and selective typographical emphasis.
- **Primary Typography & Light Elements (`#F5F0E8`):** Ivory text that delivers high readability without the clinical glare of pure white.
- **Secondary Typography & Metadata (`#A6A19A`):** Muted warm stone grey for technical camera details, subtitles, dates, and locations.
- **Subtle Surface Stroke (`rgba(198, 164, 119, 0.18)`):** Hairline champagne boundary for framing images and content blocks without visual clutter.

## Typography
Typographic pairings rely on high contrast between expressive, humanistic serif display heads and sharp, technical modern sans-serif infrastructure:

- **Playfair Display:** Carries emotional weight, timeless beauty, and editorial nuance. Used for all major narrative headers, couple names, and overarching series titles. Italicized cuts can be utilized selectively for dates, locations, or secondary clauses to evoke classic fashion publishing.
- **Manrope:** Provides clean, functional grounding. Configured with an airy letter-spacing profile and lightweight cut to allow effortless readability against dark canvases.
- **Uppercase Tracking:** Micro-labels, metadata (e.g., lens specs, film formats, destination tags), and category tags employ extended letter-spacing (`0.22em`) to echo high-end art monographs.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout reflects luxury coffee table books, utilizing an asymmetric 12-column grid system built on open space:

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column fluid grid on desktop (`margin: 4rem`, `gutter: 2rem`), reflowing to a 4-column structure on mobile devices (`margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1rem`).
- **Asymmetric Offsets:** Visual elements intentionally avoid predictable, symmetrical grids. An image portfolio pair might feature an 8-column wide cinematic frame offset against a 3-column portrait crop with staggered vertical alignments.
- **Breathing Intervals:** Generous use of `space-xl` and `space-2xl` between editorial acts, wedding chapters, and film reels to provide visual pause between dense visual narratives.

## Elevation & Depth
In alignment with darkroom and gallery traditions, visual depth avoids heavy, artificial drop-shadows. Instead, elevation is expressed through luminance transitions, translucent scrims, and delicate luminous glows:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - *Base Layer (0):* `#080808` canvas.
  - *Tier 1 (Surface):* `#101010` for photo cards, media enclosures, and client access drawers.
  - *Tier 2 (Floating Panels & Modals):* `#141414` with a 0.5px champagne border (`rgba(198, 164, 119, 0.22)`).
- **Luminous Ambient Halos:** Selected focal components (e.g., featured cinematic showreels or primary booking CTAs) cast an ultra-soft golden radiance: `box-shadow: 0 16px 48px -12px rgba(198, 164, 119, 0.12)`.
- **Glassmorphic Scrims:** Film navigation bars and floating photo headers utilize 12px background blurs over `rgba(8, 8, 8, 0.75)`.
- **Structural Outlines:** Subtle, hairline borders (`1px` solid `rgba(245, 240, 232, 0.08)`) outline media containers, creating architectural precision without drawing attention away from the photography.

## Shapes
Geometry is strictly **Sharp (`0`)**. 

- **Architectural Edge:** All framing viewports, buttons, inputs, tags, and media card containers have razor-sharp 0px borders. This mirrors medium-format film slides, printed photo borders, and classic gallery mats.
- **Frame Windows:** Visual containers favor strict cinematic aspect ratios (16:9, 2.39:1 anamorphic, 4:5 vertical portrait, and 1:1 contact sheet thumbnails).
- **Separator Accents:** Vertical and horizontal dividers exist as precise 1px linear cuts rendered in champagne-tinted transparency.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Editorial Button:** Sharp edges, solid `#C6A477` fill with `#080808` typography in `label-caps`. Hover states invert to `#F5F0E8` background with an ambient gold glow.
- **Secondary Ghost Button:** Sharp edges, transparent background, bounded by a 1px border in `rgba(198, 164, 119, 0.4)`. Text in `#F5F0E8`. Hover shifts background to `rgba(198, 164, 119, 0.08)` and border to full opacity.
- **Text Link CTA:** All-caps label with an underline separated by 6px of spacing; hover transitions evoke a smooth horizontal line expansion.

### Form Inputs & Date Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Flush, bottom-border-only design (`1px solid rgba(245, 240, 232, 0.2)`) on `#080808` canvas. When focused, border transitions smoothly to `#C6A477` with floating labels set in `label-caps`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Minimalist square enclosures (`14px x 14px`) with sharp corners, filled with champagne gold check indicators when selected.

### Media & Narrative Cards
- **Cinematic Story Card:** Sharp charcoal surface (`#101010`) housing full-bleed media. Accompanying narrative contains a category label in muted warm grey, couple title in `headline-sm`, and wedding venue/location metadata.
- **Exhibition Grid Chips:** Framed with 1px hairline border, containing event categories (e.g., *“Sangli Sangeet”*, *“Royal Maharani Portraiture”*, *“4K Anamorphic Films”*) set in `label-caps`.

### Additional Specialized Components
- **Cinematic Film Reel Player:** Custom video container featuring minimal champagne scrubber lines, anamorphic aspect ratio toggle, and unobtrusive timecodes set in `Manrope`.
- **Exhibition Lightbox:** Fullscreen pitch-black backdrop with 1px champagne boundary indicators, single-click EXIF metadata toggle (focal length, camera body, aperture), and smooth pan transitions.
- **Client Commission Drawer:** Slide-in reservation panel wrapped in `#101010` featuring customized package calculators, date availability indicators, and bespoke bespoke consultation booking.
